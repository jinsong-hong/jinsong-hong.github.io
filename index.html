Research Directions

What I am building toward

01

Observer-Based Contact Estimation

Using observer-based estimates to expose contact force, disturbance, and unmodeled dynamics to low-level controllers and learned policies.

02

Control-Aware Sim-to-Real RL

Studying how action space, actuator model, delay, domain randomization, and safety layers affect hardware transfer.

03

Compliant Physical Interaction

Comparing position-target policies, torque-level policies, impedance control, and admittance control under matched disturbance tests.

Bridging the Sim-to-Real Gap in Parallel-Link Leg Mechanisms via Simulator-Side Dynamics Normalization

Jinsong Hong, Jangho Kim, Jihwan Lee, Donghyun Kim, Sehoon Oh

Restores actuator inertia, damping, and residual linkage inertia lost when parallel-link legs are represented by serial-tree simulators. Hardware-informed Simulator-Side System Normalization (S3N) improves motion and force consistency, reducing the phase-averaged, command-normalized locomotion gap from 17.3% to 9.9%.

Design of a Cascaded Disturbance Observer for Robust Quadruped Body Orientation Control

Jinsong Hong, Changmin Yeo, Sehoon Oh

Combines an inner-loop leg disturbance observer with an outer-loop Body DOB for quadruped orientation control. The outer observer estimates roll and pitch disturbances and maps the compensation into leg radial velocity commands, while the inner observer compensates leg dynamics and friction.

Whole-Body Phase Synchronization via Period Adaptation for SLIP Driven Quadruped Robot

Changmin Yeo, Jinsong Hong, Sehoon Oh

Coordinates independently controlled SLIP legs through touchdown-triggered period adaptation. Correcting inter-leg phase drift prevents early-touchdown-induced gait collapse and maintains trunk velocity tracking in MuJoCo simulations under constant and changing speed commands.

Jangho Kim, Jihwan Lee, Jinsong Hong, Sehoon Oh
